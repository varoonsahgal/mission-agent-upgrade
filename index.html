// 01 — Mission brief

One agent. One mission. No disconnected tutorials.

This is an immersive, scenario-based workshop. You'll see how Microsoft Copilot Studio turns a basic AI assistant into a business solution: one that automates workflows, connects to enterprise systems, applies decision logic, and works inside organizational guardrails.

Before

An assistant that talks

It can answer troubleshooting questions about Contoso's digital services, but it can't do anything. Every answer ends with “…now go open a ticket yourself.”

The upgrade

Workflows, logic, connectors

Triggers, actions, conditions, and an issue-register integration give the agent the ability to investigate, decide, and act.

After

A governed business solution

Approval gates, least-privilege access, prompt-injection defenses, and tested failure paths. It's safe enough to show your leadership.

// 05 — The signature lab

Digital Service Mission Control

A Contoso Digital employee needs help investigating a problem with a member-facing digital service. Mobile app crashes? QuickPay transfers stuck? Passcodes not arriving? The starter agent can answer troubleshooting questions, but it can't take action. Not for long.

STAGE 01

Understand

Interpret the incoming issue using approved knowledge.

STAGE 02

Investigate

Check whether the issue already exists in the issue register.

STAGE 03

Decide

Choose to give guidance, collect details, or escalate.

STAGE 04

Act

Start a workflow or create an authorized tracking record.

STAGE 05

Protect

Permissions, approval gates, and exception handling.

STAGE 06

Verify

Prove the outcome through tests and observable results.

🤯

THE WOW MOMENTYou'll watch an AI agent go beyond answering a question. It runs a connected business workflow and comes back with a verifiable result, such as a real Issue ID in a real register, created through a governed process.

// 08 — Crew assignments

Every license level gets a seat in the capsule.

Activities support three participation modes, so everyone gets a real mission whatever their licensing or authoring access.

Builder

Hands-on builders

Have approved sandbox access
  • Build and test every lab in a Copilot Studio sandbox
  • Own the agent configuration and the workflows
  • Run the anomaly scenarios live
Team

Collaborative teams

Share an authorized maker account
  • Rotate roles: Solution Designer, Builder, and Risk Reviewer
  • One person drives while two navigate and challenge
  • Present as a team at the showcase
Observer

Observer / analysts

No authoring access needed
  • Design challenges and decision mapping
  • Follow along with the guided demos and screenshot walkthroughs
  • Lead troubleshooting and test-case design

All labs use fictional data and approved classroom resources. Live builds depend on having the right Copilot Studio authoring permissions and connector access.
