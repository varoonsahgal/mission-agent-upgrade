// Crew roles

Team roles for collaborative mode

Rotate roles at each break so everyone drives at least once.

📐
Role 01

Solution Designer

Owns the what and the why. Reads the mission brief, defines inputs and outputs, decides the branches, and keeps the team on scope.

🛠
Role 02

Builder

Owns the keyboard. Configures the agent, topics, flows, and connectors in Copilot Studio, and narrates each click aloud.

🛡
Role 03

Risk Reviewer

Owns the “what could go wrong?” question. Challenges permissions, writes the edge-case tests, keeps score, and has the final call in games.

// Abort procedures

Contingency plans

A learner has no Copilot Studio license or access

Move them to a team or the Observer track right away. Each lab's screenshot walkthrough lets them follow every step. Pair them with a builder and make them the Risk Reviewer, which is the role with the most leverage.

SharePoint list creation is blocked

Share the pre-built instructor register with Edit permissions, or have teams share one list. As a last resort, demo Mission 06 from the reference agent and have learners complete the connection audit.

A connector is blocked by a data policy

Perfect teaching moment. Show the error, explain data policies (Mission 08 preview), and continue on the reference environment.

Approvals don't arrive

Check the Approvals app in Teams and the Outlook inbox of the assigned approver. Approvals can take a minute to appear. Keep a recorded run ready to show what should happen.

A lab runs long

Each lab has a core path and a stretch path. Cut the stretch path, and keep the telemetry check: it's how learners know they succeeded.

The agent responds unexpectedly in the test pane

Start a new test conversation, check the Activity view to see which topic or tool was chosen, and review the tool and topic descriptions. Most surprises come from vague descriptions.

Reset between cohorts Delete test records created in the register (anything other than DSI-1041 to 1047), remove the Rogue Document from any shared agent, restore the 403-demo connection, and clear any pending approvals.
